# LOVE ALL
## A Nancy Tennis Club

**Pleasure is the point.**

Creative direction · 4 September 2026 · Local concept, not a published campaign or an actual Gentle Monster collaboration.

## The decision

Build a tennis club in which the rules of competition quietly give way to the rules of pleasure. Everything looks exact: the court lines, the uniforms, the chrome, the rituals. Then something extraordinarily soft happens.

Two three-meter tennis balls lean together over a net. Their surfaces compress at the point of contact. The net yields. Nobody wins the point. Nobody wants it to end.

This sculpture is **The Embrace**. It is the recognizable image of LOVE ALL, the opening shot of the campaign, the anchor of a possible installation, and the recurring mark of the season. One strong image, made worth returning to.

The creative leap is to make Nancy's tennis collection feel like evidence that this club exists. Ace and Smash become the equipment you can take home from it.

## What the website is doing today

I inspected the live homepage and the current tennis collection and product pages. This is an editorial and visual assessment, not a claim about conversion performance; no analytics were reviewed.

There is already real equity here. The homepage leads with “Game. Set. O.”, a pink tennis setting, the two products, and packaging with stripes and sporting language. The collection explicitly says everyone wins and nobody keeps score. Smash's packaging carries the phrase “Where Love Means Everything.” LOVE ALL extends these existing ideas rather than replacing them with an unrelated fashion concept.

The homepage currently asks the tennis hero to do several jobs at once: a promotion, reassurance, beginner education, review proof, and two different shopping directions. Immediately afterward, the page returns to the general bestsellers before introducing the tennis collection again. The long continuation mixes testimonials, education, other products, and detailed selling sections. The result is a tennis campaign sitting on top of a much broader sales page.

Keep the recognizable Nancy identity, actual products, confident color, clear shopping path, and useful reassurance. Give the tennis story a coherent beginning, middle, and end. Let one image create desire; let the next section answer what the products are.

## What to learn from Gentle Monster

The useful lesson is the continuity between a campaign's imaginary world, its objects, and the things people can actually buy. The official Jentle Salon presentation connects a dreamlike campaign with a collection and customizable charms. My interpretation: the world makes the objects culturally interesting, and the objects let people participate in the world.

For Nancy, this suggests a signature sculptural gesture, carefully controlled materials, a film with its own physics, and collectible details that extend the same idea. It does not require copying existing installations or mimicking a competitor's typography. This is an independent Nancy concept, not a proposed announcement of a collaboration.

## Three possible moves

| Direction | What it buys us | What it leaves on the table |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grand Slam campaign refresh | Fast: stronger tennis photography and a tighter collection page. | Still a seasonal campaign. Little reason to explore or remember a world. |
| **LOVE ALL — recommended** | A distinct place, recognizable sculpture, product story, film, and culture system from one idea. | Requires art direction and disciplined continuity. |
| After-hours performance club | A provocative dark, chrome, fashion-led capsule. | Pulls Nancy away from its bright, welcoming character and toward an already crowded luxury aesthetic. |

Choose LOVE ALL. Make the execution audacious, and the proposition easy to understand.

## The world's one physical rule

**Precision meets softness.**

Everything obeys realistic light, weight, texture, and architecture, except one deliberate event in each scene. Felt can gently compress beyond expectation. A ball can rest where an official should sit. A rigid-looking net can relax. No floating assortment of unrelated surreal props. No generic candyland. No cartoon faces.

The mood is an adult invitation: intelligent, tactile, playful, a little strange. The camera observes the absurdity with complete composure.

## A club with three rooms

### 01 — Center Court / The Embrace

The emotional entry. A pink and an optic-lime tennis ball meet above a net on a court that is otherwise exact. The balls feel enormous because the net, lines, and one ordinary ball stay human-scale. Their contact is tender, not explosive. Keep the net readable: it is the boundary that yields.

Copy: **LOVE ALL. Pleasure is the point.**

The first frame must work as a still poster. The film earns the second look when the viewer notices the pressure changing. On a possible physical installation, visitors can walk around the sculpture and discover the tiny normal ball. The product display is nearby; it is never necessary to solve a puzzle to shop.

### 02 — The Equipment Room / Objects for a different kind of play

Actual Ace and Smash become precise objects of desire. Product forms, colorways, apertures, buttons, seams, branding, and packaging remain faithful to the supplied official photography. Architectural echoes are allowed; redesigning the products in a generated image is not.

**Ace:** introduce the compact tennis-ball form and air-pulse sensation in plain language. **Smash:** introduce the tennis-ball head and external wand format. Avoid requiring tennis knowledge to understand either product.

Image direction: a single object on a chalk plinth beside a taut green string detail; a second crop reveals the material and controls. A shared, measured camera position gives the pair the character of equipment in a specialist collection. The existing colorful packaging is a strength—show the real boxes in an unboxing sequence rather than inventing a new package.

No abstract film should imply that the products change shape, stretch, float, self-move, or have features they do not have. Product shots are distinct from world sculptures. Use official photographs until a generated frame survives side-by-side fidelity review.

### 03 — The Clubhouse / An official day off

A giant tennis ball is slumped over the chrome umpire chair. There is no official keeping score. This is the quieter second expression of the same soft physics, not a competing campaign mascot.

Deep-green lacquer lockers, pink terrazzo, hard sunlight, almost no props. Editorial stories can live here: choosing a first toy, taking your time, play without performance. Existing education and support stay useful and plainly named.

Copy: **No one is keeping score. Take your time.**

Possible future culture objects: an oversized towel, a ball-shaped bag charm, a numbered club card, a folded rulebook with three rules—be curious, go at your pace, stop when you want. These are proposals, not existing products or promised launches. Prototype one desirable object before expanding to a merchandise range.

## The film: THE POINT, 15 seconds

One restrained physical event. The full cut would be edited from controlled shots, not generated as a frantic multi-scene montage.

| Time | Picture | Sound / intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Low, wide view. Two monumental balls remain almost still on opposite sides of the net. A small ball settles near the foreground line. | A solitary bounce, then space. Establish scale. |
| 3–6s | Tight crop of white net tape gradually giving under a little weight. | A fine thread creak. Create anticipation. |
| 6–10s | Pink and lime felt make contact. The compression is modest, tactile, slow. | A soft felt brush. The decisive moment is connection. |
| 10–13s | Return to the wide. Hold the embrace. Architecture remains perfectly still. | Exhale of air, no voice needed. Let the viewer look. |
| 13–15s | Typeset end card, added in editing: LOVE ALL / A Nancy Tennis Club. | One clean club bell. Finish gently. |

The homepage uses a shorter silent loop with no baked-in text. A visible pause control and still fallback are required. Respect reduced-motion preference; never make sound automatic. Social cuts can use sound by deliberate playback. Generate true portrait compositions for mobile instead of sacrificing both balls to an indiscriminate crop.

### Motion-test prompt

Use the approved Embrace master as the image reference. Preserve exactly the architecture, two ball identities, seam paths, net geometry, court lines, lighting, and composition. Locked camera with a nearly imperceptible forward push. Over six seconds, the pink and optic-lime felt tennis balls lean very slightly toward one another and softly compress at their existing contact point; the net dips a few centimeters, then they settle. Keep the balls solid and physically weighted, maintain meticulous felt fibers, and keep all background geometry perfectly static. Realistic material movement, quiet contemporary sculpture film, hard late-afternoon sun. No cuts, text, logos, people, new objects, bouncing, bursting, melting, morphing, dramatic camera orbit, or sudden lighting changes. End on a held embrace. Silent.

This is a test of material behavior, not a promise of a seamless final loop. Reject seam drift, shape melting, extra props, flickering court lines, or a net passing through a ball. Trim to a stable section if necessary; do not hide a broken transformation with frantic cutting.

## The homepage, rebuilt around the world

1. **Arrival.** Nancy identity, a visible Shop Tennis link, and the sculpture film. LOVE ALL / Pleasure is the point. One primary action: Shop the collection. Exploring the world is the quieter second path.
2. **Equipment, immediately.** Ace and Smash together, actual photographs, clear product names, plain descriptions, live prices drawn from commerce data, and direct product links. Place this within the next viewport so the brand film does not postpone comprehension.
3. **The invitation.** One short sentence connects pleasure without performance to the scene. The tactile macro acts as a visual pause.
4. **Inside the club.** A single clubhouse chapter and a short education story. Keep exploration optional, keyboard accessible, and readable without motion.
5. **Confidence.** Product-specific proof, delivery/packaging details, returns and support taken from verified current policy. Compress repeated reassurance; do not discard it.
6. **Departure.** A strong collection image and a direct shopping action. The general Nancy range remains available through navigation and a modest collection link.

Do not move urgency language into the art direction. If a real promotion is active, give it one factual announcement strip and an accurate end time. Do not invent scarcity, future partnerships, sell-through claims, testimonials, or launch dates.

### The craft gate

- Does it read as a distinctive tennis world at thumbnail size?
- Is there one event rather than an assortment of visual tricks?
- Are texture, contact shadows, seams, scale, and weight convincing?
- Can the product remain recognizable without being confused with a fictional sculpture?
- Does the family share light, materials, architecture, and an attitude?
- Can a new customer identify what is for sale and reach it immediately?
- Does it still feel warm, adult, and unmistakably Nancy?

## Moving image × live typography — 5 September

The physical world moves in the film. Typography, navigation, and interaction stay in the browser. Every scene is composed with quiet regions for copy; words are never burned into the generated footage.

Three working treatments now share one Higgsfield Embrace film in the opening. These are selectable demonstrations of a direction; a final storefront would choose one treatment for each place in the journey.

1. **In the court.** The felt balls lean, compress, and release. Oversized solid LOVE and outline ALL sit over the physical world. The shop link stays immediately available. Best as the main arrival.
2. **Through the type.** LOVE ALL becomes a window into the moving court. Live HTML lettering and CSS screen compositing reveal the film only through the letterforms. Switching back opens the frame. Best as a chapter threshold or a dramatic campaign reveal.
3. **At your touch.** Press and hold to advance the Embrace to its contact moment. Let go and the clip completes its release. SOFT compresses and its F tilts in response to the same frame timeline. Space and Enter work, too. Best as one optional tactile moment after the visitor understands the world.

Next scene ideas, not generated in this pass:

- **The line gives.** A tight locked shot of the ivory net tape taking tension, then relaxing. Align a thin HTML divider with the tape, then carry that line into the Equipment Room as a scroll transition. Motion belongs to the net; product names and controls remain sharp.
- **The official exhales.** The collapsed felt sculpture settles a little farther into the chrome umpire chair. Live NO PRESSURE type slowly opens its spacing while the camera and lockers remain completely still. Give this scene silence and breathing room.
- **The court becomes the object.** End a macro felt shot on a still frame, then reveal an exact official Ace photograph in the same color field. Animate the layout and image frame rather than morphing the real product into an invented shape.

The current mobile layout reframes the wide clip within a smaller stage to retain both balls. A separate portrait Higgsfield generation is a future asset; the existing portrait still and authored 21-second camera edit remain available. The new film is silent, pauses outside the viewport, and starts still for reduced-motion users until they request playback.
