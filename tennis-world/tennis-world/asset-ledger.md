### M01 prompt, ready for schema-appropriate submission

Use the supplied Embrace image as the exact visual reference. Preserve the architecture, the pink and optic-lime tennis-ball identities, seam paths, net geometry, court lines, lighting, and composition. One continuous five-second shot, locked camera with a nearly imperceptible forward push. The two monumental felt tennis balls lean very slightly toward one another and softly compress at their existing contact point, then settle. Keep the net between them, with a tiny physically credible give; do not pull the tape through either ball. Keep the balls solid and physically weighted, maintain meticulous felt fibers, and keep all background geometry perfectly static. Quiet contemporary sculpture film, realistic material movement, hard late-afternoon sun. No cuts, text, logos, people, new objects, bouncing, bursting, melting, morphing, dramatic camera orbit, or lighting changes. End on a held embrace. Silent.

This first test starts from a frame with existing contact. It tests settling and compression; it does not attempt to manufacture the full pre-contact opening. A complete 15-second cut requires separate anticipation and net-detail plates after this motion test passes.
